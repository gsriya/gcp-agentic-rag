# Northwind Retail – Travel and Expenses Policy (fictional sample data)

## Booking travel

All business travel must be booked through the company travel portal at least 14 days in advance. Train travel is preferred for journeys under 4 hours. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is £180 in London and £130 elsewhere in the UK. International limits are listed in the travel portal.

## Meals and receipts

The daily meal allowance is £45 when travelling. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Every expense over £25 needs an itemised receipt.

## Submitting claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims are approved by the line manager and paid with the next monthly payroll.
