# Northwind Retail – IT Security Policy (fictional sample data)

## Passwords and sign-in

All staff accounts must use multi-factor authentication (MFA). Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are rotated every 180 days. Password managers approved by IT are allowed; sharing passwords is never allowed.

## Laptops and devices

Company laptops are encrypted with full-disk encryption. A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to the IT service desk within 2 hours. Personal devices may access email only through the managed mobile app.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer payment data is Restricted and must never be stored on laptops or sent by email. Confidential data may be shared externally only through the approved secure file-sharing tool.

## Incident reporting

Suspected security incidents, including phishing emails, must be reported to security@northwind.example within 30 minutes of discovery. The security team acknowledges every report within 1 hour during business hours.
